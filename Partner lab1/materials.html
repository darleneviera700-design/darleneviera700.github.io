Gather Your Watercolor Tools

Home Materials Tutorials

Watercolor painting requires a few essential materials to achieve the best results. Beginners should start with quality watercolor paints (Winsor & Newton), watercolor paper and a variety of brushes designed for different painting techniques. Watercolor paper is specially made to absorb water without becoming damaged, while quality brushes help create both fine details and broad washes of color. Having the proper materials allows artists to develop their skills, experiment with new techniques, and create vibrant works of art with confidence!

Here Is a List to Get You Started on Your Watercolor Journey!

  • Watercolor paints
  • Watercolor paper
  • Paintbrushes
  • A cup of clean water
  • Paper towels
  • A mixing palette

The Story Behind My Watercolor Portrait of American Psycho

I was inspired to create this artwork as a Christmas gift for my son, something meaningful that he could cherish for years to come. American Psycho is one of his favoriate films, and he greatly admires the actor featured in it. To create a more striking visual effect, I chose to render the portrait primarily in black and white while highlighting the blood on his face in red. Rather than using a full-color palette, I felt that the contrast between the monochromatic tones and the vivid red would create a more dramatic and eye-catching composition. This artistic choice emphasizes the intensity of the character while drawing the viewer's attention to the focal point of the piece.

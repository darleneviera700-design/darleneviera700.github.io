The Bermuda Triangle

The Bermuda Triangle is an area of the Atlanta Ocean between Florida, Bermuda, and Puerto Rico. It became famous because of stories involving missing ships and aircrafts.

  • Some believe the lost city of Atlantis lies beneath.
  • Flight 19 disappeared in the area in 1945.
  • Abandoned ships found drifting without their crews have fueled legends.

Evidence File

Flight 19

Five Navy aircrafts disappeared during a training mission on December 5, 1945.

Possible Explanations

Trapped Methane Hydrate

A popular theory suffests that massive methane gas bubbles rising from the ocean floor may have caused ships and aircrafts to wanish without warning.

Magnetic Veriance

Some believe unusual magnetic fields interfere with compasses, causing ships and aircrafts to lose their way leading pilots and sailors off course.

Rogue Waves

Some researches believe towering rogue waves could explain the sudden disappearance of ships in the Bermuda Triangle.
